    How it works

    Patient Guidance

    A simple four-step process from registration to consultation.

    01

    Register Digitally

    Create your account, fill in the registration form and choose your department.

    02

    Receive Your Token

    Get an instant, unique queue token and print or save your receipt.

    03

    Track Your Position

    Follow your queue position and estimated wait on your token dashboard.

    04

    Visit the Department

    Head to your department when your token shows as Serving.

    SmartCare handles registration and queue guidance only. It does not provide diagnosis or emergency care. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
